The coach

Every move gets a grade. Bad ones get a reason.

The grade is never a guess. It comes from how much winning chance your move gave away compared with the engine's best move.

The engine judges

A built-in alpha-beta engine analyses every position in the background. When you move, it compares your move with its best one and grades it. No internet needed.

Hints when stuck

Bring your own model

Use the AI you already like.

Pick a provider in Settings, paste your key, press Test connection. Base URL and model are editable, so any proxy or gateway that speaks the Anthropic or OpenAI format works too.

ClaudeOpenAIGeminiOpenRouterOllama on your MacAny compatible proxy
  • Your key stays in your Keychain. It is never written to disk or logs.
  • Only the position and moves are sent. Requests go straight from your Mac to your provider.
  • Optional. Grades, hints and the evaluation bar work with no key at all.
♚︎
Versus AI model

Play against your favourite model.

Pick a model and a colour. The app sends it the position and the full list of legal moves. If it answers with an illegal move it gets two more tries; after that the engine plays that one move for it and tells you so.

Prefer a classic opponent? The built-in computer has Easy, Medium and Hard levels, and two people can play on one Mac.

Installing

  1. Open the disk image and drag Chess into Applications.
  2. The app is not notarised by Apple, so the first launch is blocked. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Or run this once in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Chess.app
  4. For AI features, open Chess → Settings (⌘,) and add a key. Everything else works without one.
